Aurora & Anna — Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of information associated with your Instagram interaction, contact ZeroZeroComputer using the official contact details at zerozerocomputer.it.

Include your Instagram username and state that the request concerns the “Aurora & Anna” application. Do not include passwords or access tokens. We will verify the request, delete or anonymize matching application data, and confirm completion through the contact channel used for the request.

You can also delete messages in Instagram and revoke the application's access from your Instagram/Meta account settings.
